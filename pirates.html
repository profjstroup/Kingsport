PIRATES!a golden‑age caribbean adventure

Take command of your ship upon the Spanish Main. Trade, plunder, and battle across a scattering of islands and colonial ports. Sail with W/S to trim sail speed, A/D to turn, fire cannons with Q (port) / E (starboard), and dock at any port by sailing close and pressing F. Grapple alongside a hostile ship — or bombard a town's defenses into ruin — and press B to board her or storm ashore, dueling the enemy captain or garrison commander blade to blade. Win, and you capture the ship or sack the town instead of merely sinking or bombarding it. But beware: cannon fire against a town wins you no friends, and its batteries will return fire once you've made an enemy of the Crown that flies above it. Keep an ear open in the tavern, too — word of a heavily escorted Spanish Treasure Fleet or an overland silver train passing through some unlucky port means a fortune for whoever is bold enough to seize it.
